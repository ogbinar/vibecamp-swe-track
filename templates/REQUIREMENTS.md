# Product requirement brief

## Problem and users

Describe the user outcome, current pain, constraints, and what is intentionally out of scope. Avoid prescribing a tool.

## Stakeholders and decision rights

Name affected roles, the decision owner, and any conflicting outcome or constraint. For a solo/synthetic exercise, label roles as assumptions; do not invent meeting transcripts.

## Observable examples

| Given | When | Then | Failure semantics |
|---|---|---|---|

## Invariants and risks

List truths that must hold across API, database, retries, concurrency, and authorization as applicable.

## Smallest correct slice

State the first end-to-end behavior and how it will be tested, deliberately broken, operated, and shipped.

## Open decisions

Only promote a decision to an ADR if it is costly to reverse or materially affects contract, security, infrastructure, or operations.

## Requirement change record

| Original example/assumption | New request or evidence | Accepted interpretation and owner | Rejected scope | Test/evidence changed |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Record why acceptance changed before implementation. A stakeholder brief is useful only when it changes observable behavior, priority, or constraints.
